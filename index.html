About the game

A small arcade game that keeps growing

Wolf And Eggs 3D starts with a simple idea: move your hero, catch the eggs and try to beat your best score. Around that core I have added runner stages, a persistent world, local Bluetooth multiplayer and built-in mini-games such as Memory and Checkers.

The game is designed around gameplay rather than monetization. There are no advertising interruptions and no paid power-ups that decide who performs better. If someone chooses to support the project, it is a voluntary way to help development continue.

About NextPulse Games

One creator, from the first prototype to every update

NextPulse Games is a one-person game project. Wolf And Eggs 3D is designed, programmed, tested and developed independently, with Godot Engine handling the game and Blender supporting the 3D work. The goal is straightforward: make family-friendly games that are enjoyable to play without building the experience around advertisements or pay-to-win purchases.

I keep improving the project from real play tests and player feedback. New features are added when they make the game clearer, more varied or simply more fun.
